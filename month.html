Book of the month

One book we think deserves a place on your shelf this month.

Anxious People

by Fredrik Backman

A bank robbery goes wrong, and the would-be robber runs into an apartment viewing and takes everyone there hostage. When the police finally get inside, the robber has vanished, and they have to work out who these very different strangers really are.

  • funny
  • hopeful
  • found family
Find more like this

Why we picked it

A failed bank robbery sounds like the start of a thriller, but this book is much more interested in the people than in the crime. Every hostage has a story, and the author slowly shows why each of them is more fragile than they look.

It is a good pick when you want something funny that also lets you feel things. The jokes are sharp, the characters argue and misunderstand each other, and somewhere along the way a group of strangers starts to look after one another.

It deals with difficult subjects, such as loneliness and money worries, with a lot of kindness, so you finish it feeling hopeful and not heavy. It does touch on depression, so keep that in mind if you are in a fragile mood.
