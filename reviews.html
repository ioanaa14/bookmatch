What readers think

Six of Crows

★★★★★

I wanted a heist and I got a crew I would follow anywhere. Everyone has a past that comes out slowly, and the plan keeps changing in ways I didn’t expect. The first chapters are a bit confusing because of all the names, but it is worth pushing through.

Review by Andreea

Gone Girl

★★★★☆

I couldn't put it down and the twist in the middle surprised me. It loses a star because the ending left me annoyed and did not meet my expectations.

Review by Andrei

Persuasion

★★★★☆

Quieter than Pride and Prejudice, but I liked it more. The start is slow, but Anne is so patient and sensible that you end up on her side from the first chapters. The letter near the end is worth the whole wait.

Review by Mara
